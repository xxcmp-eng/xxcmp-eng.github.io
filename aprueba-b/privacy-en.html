Privacy Policy

Aprueba B (es.apruebab.app) · Last updated: 11 September 2026 · Español · Русский

In short. The app runs entirely on your phone. There is no account, no developer server, no ads and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app leaves the device.

Controller

Ivan Diakov (independent developer). Contact: ivan.diakov@gmail.com.

Data processed

  • Study progress (answers, exams, streak, achievements, settings) is stored only on your device in a local database; it is never sent anywhere. You can export it as a backup, import it on another device, or delete it (Settings → Account → Reset progress, or by uninstalling).
  • Daily reminder: a locally scheduled notification; no push services or device identifiers.
  • Content: questions, explanations and translations ship inside the app and are not fetched from the internet.
  • Account: the current version has no sign-in or cloud sync.

Ads and purchases

The current version shows no ads and initialises no advertising SDK. Should a future version enable them, this policy would be updated first and the app would ask for the consent required in the EEA/UK. In-app purchases, if any, are handled by Google Play; the developer receives no payment data.

Not collected

Name, email, location, contacts, photos, user or advertising identifiers, app usage. No first- or third-party analytics, no crash reporting.

Children

The app prepares for the category B licence exam (minimum age 18) and is intended for adults; it is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure or objection at the contact above; since the data lives only on your device, you can view, export and delete it yourself from the app.

Changes

Changes will be published on this page with the update date.
